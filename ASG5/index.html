How it works

Explore a floating crystal island made with Three.js. Click the five glowing crystals to charge the center beacon, activate the portal, and unlock the treasure chest.

The world includes a day/night switch, camera tour, animated beacon effects, floating particles, clouds, planets, constellations, meteors, shadows, textured objects, a skybox, and a loaded GLB model.
